Re-entry

Warm-up · eight minutes
  • Calf raises — 2 × 15
  • Supported toe raises — 1 × 10, back and head against a wall
  • Glute bridges — 2 × 12, slow
  • Banded lateral steps — 2 × 15 each way
  • Banded terminal knee extension — 1 × 15 each leg
  • Band pull-apart — 2 × 15
  • Chin nods — 1 × 8, five-second holds
  • Wall slides — 1 × 10
The rules
  • Never lock out. Knees or elbows, stop just shy of straight.
  • Lower slowly. Three seconds down on everything.
  • No weight on the knee work. It grows by seconds and depth.
  • Earn depth. You can reach positions you cannot yet control.
  • Keep breathing. Out on the effort. No held breath, no grinding reps.
  • No shrug. Shoulders stay away from the ears on every lift and carry.
  • Swap, do not stack. Where a card offers another movement, pick one for the week. Each keeps its own weights.
  • One change at a time. More weight, more reps or a new movement, never two at once.
  • Hold when it talks back. A day-after score of 2 or lower, or a headache after training, means repeat the week at last week’s weights.
Stop signs
  • End the session for a headache that builds as you lift, new neck pain, dizziness or a change in vision.
  • Get emergency care for a sudden severe headache, neck pain unlike your usual, a drooping face, weakness, numbness or trouble speaking.

Neck and posture

Five minutes, every day, gym or not. Gentle enough that you could do it twice.

Desk, couch and bed
  • Screen at eye level. A laptop stand and a separate keyboard at the desk.
  • Couch or bed. A lap desk, a pillow behind the low back, thirty minutes at a time.
  • Move often. Stand and walk every 30 to 45 minutes. A changed position beats a perfect one.
Leave these out
  • Neck circles and hard ear-to-shoulder stretches.
  • Anyone cracking or manipulating your neck.
  • Stretching tight traps for relief. They want strength under them, not a longer stretch.
  • Headstands, shoulder stands and loaded neck positions.
