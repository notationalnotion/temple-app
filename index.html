Warm-up · six minutes
  • Calf raises — 2 × 15
  • Glute bridges — 2 × 12, slow
  • Banded lateral steps — 2 × 15 each way
  • Banded terminal knee extension — 1 × 15 each leg
  • Band pull-apart — 2 × 15
The four rules
  • Never lock out. Knees or elbows, stop just shy of straight.
  • Lower slowly. Three seconds down on everything.
  • No weight on the knee work. It grows by seconds and depth.
  • Earn depth. You can reach positions you cannot yet control.
